Method, rules and caveats
  • Trypsin follows the Keil rule (C-terminal to K/R, not before P). Trypsin/P ignores the proline rule, closer to search-engine defaults.
  • Lys-C: after K. Arg-C: after R, not before P. Glu-C: after E (bicarbonate). Asp-N: before D. Chymotrypsin (high specificity): after F/W/Y/L, not before P.
  • Masses are monoisotopic and unmodified (no carbamidomethyl-C or oxidation). Peptides with X, B, Z are dropped.
  • Isoform collapsing needs a gene tag in the header (gene=, gene: or GN=); without one every sequence is kept, and shared peptides between isoforms lower the "unique" counts.
  • This is a theoretical upper bound: ionisation, retention and PTMs decide what an instrument actually sees.
  • The page opens on a synthetic demo set (random sequences with vertebrate-like composition). Load the Echinobase file for real numbers.
  • For full per-protein tables, use the companion script sp_digest/sp_digest.py; it applies the same rules.
